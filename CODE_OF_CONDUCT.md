# BYOP Code of Conduct

Bring Your Own Prompt is a practical learning space for people with different backgrounds, roles, tools, and levels of experience.

Participants agree to:

- Treat unfinished work and honest questions with respect
- Critique prompts and outputs without attacking people
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and demeaning behavior
- Remove confidential, personal, regulated, or proprietary information before sharing
- Disclose relevant affiliations and avoid promotional pitches
- Make room for others to contribute

This code applies to BYOP sessions, repository activity, and other community spaces. Organizers may redirect, mute, remove, or exclude participants whose behavior makes the space unsafe or repeatedly undermines its purpose.

Until a private organizer contact is published, use a repository issue only for non-sensitive conduct questions; never post a private report publicly.
