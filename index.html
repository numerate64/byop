Why BYOP

Prompts get better
when they leave your screen.

Most prompt advice is generic. Real prompts have a job to do, context to respect, and an output someone actually needs.

BYOP is a welcoming room to show the work, compare approaches, and learn the reusable patterns hiding inside one another’s prompts.

The weekly workshop

Show. Test.
Remix.

No lectures. No “perfect prompt” theater. Just a focused hour of practical work with people who are curious enough to share what they’re trying.

  1. 01
    SHOW

    Name the job

    What are you trying to accomplish, who is it for, and where does the current prompt fall short?

    10 min
  2. 02
    TEST

    Run the prompt

    We look at the result, spot ambiguity, surface hidden assumptions, and decide what to change.

    35 min
  3. 03
    REMIX

    Keep the pattern

    We capture the improved prompt and the transferable idea everyone can use in their own work.

    15 min

Good questions

Prompt us.

Who is BYOP for?+

Anyone using—or trying to use—AI for real work. Beginners, experts, builders, leaders, creatives, and thoughtful skeptics are all welcome.

Do I have to bring a prompt?+

No. You can listen, ask questions, or help improve someone else’s prompt. Curiosity is enough for your first visit.

Which AI tools do you cover?+

BYOP is tool-agnostic. The emphasis is on clear thinking and transferable patterns, not loyalty to a particular model or vendor.

Can I share confidential work?+

No. Remove personal, confidential, regulated, or proprietary information before sharing any prompt or output.

When does BYOP meet?+

The recurring day and time will be announced before launch. Sessions will run weekly for approximately 60 minutes on Zoom.

The prompt doesn’t need to be ready

That’s the point.

Bring the version you have. Leave with a better one—and a pattern you can use again.
